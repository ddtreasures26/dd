Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

DD_Treasure26 (“we”, “us”) is a small handmade-jewellery shop. This policy explains what information the DD_Treasure26 website and app (the “Service”) collects, why, and the choices you have. We keep it simple because we collect very little.

Information we collect

  • Google account details – if you choose “Sign in with Google”, we receive your name, email address and profile photo. We do not receive your password and we do not access your Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google data.
  • Order details – the items you order, your name, phone number and, for courier delivery, your delivery address, city and PIN code, plus any note you add.
  • Reviews – the rating, name and text you submit.
  • On your device – your cart, recently viewed products, theme choice, notification preference and, for now, your orders are stored in your browser’s local storage. This stays on your device.

How we use it

  • To identify you when you sign in and show you your own orders.
  • To process, confirm, pack and deliver your order, and contact you about it (including on WhatsApp).
  • To publish reviews you submit, showing the name you entered.
  • To keep the shop running and prevent misuse.

We do not sell your information, do not show advertising and do not use it for profiling.

Where it is stored

Shop data (products, orders, reviews) is stored in a Google Drive account owned by the shop and accessible only to the shop owner. The Service is hosted on Cloudflare, which delivers the website files and may process technical data such as IP addresses to do so.

Payments

We do not process or store payment card or bank details. You pay by UPI (for example GPay) directly to the shop’s account using a QR code, outside this Service. You then send us the order details and payment screenshot on WhatsApp, which is governed by WhatsApp’s own privacy policy.

Sharing

We share your delivery name, phone number and address with the courier only as needed to deliver your order. We do not share your information with anyone else except where required by law.

Google user data

The Service’s use of information received from Google is limited to signing you in (name, email address, profile photo). We use it only to provide the features described above and do not transfer it to others, use it for advertising, or allow humans to read it except with your consent, for security, or to comply with law.

Notifications

Notifications are optional and only sent if you enable them in the app’s settings. You can turn them off at any time in the app or your browser settings.

Retention and your choices

We keep order records for as long as needed for delivery, support and our own accounts. You can sign out at any time, clear locally stored data through your browser settings, and ask us to access, correct or delete your information (including your orders and reviews) by emailing us. We will respond within a reasonable time.

Children

The Service is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above. Continued use of the Service means you accept the updated policy.

Contact
